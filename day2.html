Day 2

We got a jump start to our day at 6 A.M. because we were woken up by a man yelling outside our car, so we thought it would be best to get out of there ASAP.

Because of our early start, we were ahead of schedule to get to our first stop, so we stopped at a beach while it was at low tide. We walked around the rocks and found starfish and sea urchins.

Next, we went to our first planned stop, the Prehistoric Gardens. This is a quick walk around a trail that has life-size statues of different dinosaurs with facts about them.

Then we moved up the coast, stopping at two different lighthouses. Cape Blanco was the most memorable one because it had an attached historical house tour at the Hughes House. We had a tour guide who had every fact and her script memorized to a T. Biggest lesson from this stop: have your house built by a Swedish man and it will last over 100 years.

We drove further up through Oregon and stopped at the dunes. We could only find a small area that didn't have dune buggies racing through, so after we spent a bit of time walking around, we stopped and watched the buggies race each other.

Finally, we found a beach to park at and had dinner while watching the sunset. After this, we found a spot down a logging road and set up the truck for our second night on the road.
